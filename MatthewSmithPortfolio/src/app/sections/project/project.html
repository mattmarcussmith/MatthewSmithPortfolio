Workforce Operations Platform

Status: In Progress

Workforce Operations Platform is a portfolio project built around a realistic internal business need: helping HR and department managers centralize workforce data, track employee status, manage leave visibility, and control role-based access.

The purpose of the project is to demonstrate practical full-stack capability by solving a business problem that reflects common enterprise application patterns employers expect: secure APIs, structured data, validation, authorization, reporting views, and maintainable Angular integration.
